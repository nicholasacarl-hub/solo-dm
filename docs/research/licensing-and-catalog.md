# Licensing, naming, and the 2024-rules catalog

> Research notes gathered on 2026-11-06 while designing Solo DM. Sources are cited inline; verify before relying on them.

**Method note:** I used WebSearch only. WebFetch couldn't reach www.dndbeyond.com or nemo-illusionist.github.io because this session's network allowlist blocks them. The quoted text below therefore comes from search extracts of the official pages and PDF. Check it word for word against the PDF before shipping.

## 1. SRD 5.2 / 5.2.1
- **Dates and license:** SRD 5.2 came out 2025-04-22 and 5.2.1 on 2025-05-01. Both are CC-BY-4.0 only, with no OGL. Wizards says the license is irrevocable and that every future SRD will be CC-BY-4.0. https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd · https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1949-you-can-now-publish-your-own-creations-using-the
- **Official PDFs:**
  - 5.2.1: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf
  - 5.2: …/SRD_CC_v5.2.pdf
  - Spanish 5.2.1: …/SP_SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf
- **Required attribution (5.2.1 legal page):** "This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode."
  - The same page adds: "Please do not include any other attribution to Wizards or its parent or affiliates other than that provided above."
  - CC-BY-4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) also requires saying when the material was modified. Converting it to JSON counts.
- **What 5.2.1 changed from 5.2:**
  - Added 15 magic items that were left out of 5.2 by mistake (for example Cloak of Invisibility, Hat of Many Spells, Potion of Longevity).
  - Fixed Thunderous Greatclub.
  - Replaced a duplicated Iron Golem stat block with the Knight (only one source confirms this).
  - Added the Octopus stat block.
  - https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/general-discussion/219273-official-srd-5-2-discussion-thread?comment=68
- **Unverified:** I found no SRD 5.2.2 as of today.

## 2. Naming, trademark, AI
- **Endorsed phrasing:** The SRD legal text says you may describe your work as "compatible with fifth edition" or "5E compatible." These are the only two phrasings it approves.
- **Trademarks are not licensed:** CC-BY-4.0 §2(b)(2) excludes trademark rights. D&D Beyond lists Dungeons & Dragons, D&D, the dragon ampersand, and all Wizards product names, settings and logos as trademarks.
  - Keep them out of the app name, logo and marketing.
  - "D&D 2024" and "5.5e" are not addressed anywhere, so avoid them in branding.
  - SRD 5.2 renamed or dropped trademark-sensitive content (Strahd, Orcus). Bundle only names that appear in the SRD.
- **Unverified:** I couldn't confirm whether "Dungeon Master" is a registered Wizards trademark. "GM" is the safer word for a product name.
- **Fan Content Policy** (https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy):
  - The SRD page says CC-BY doesn't cover fan content such as streams and fan art; this policy does.
  - It requires the content to be free, bans Wizards logos and trademarks without permission, and requires an "unofficial Fan Content… Not approved/endorsed by Wizards" notice.
  - The Fan Site Kit says it does not allow publishing "free-to-use games, modules or applications" (https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/fan-site-kit).
  - Conclusion: build only on the CC-BY SRD, and use no Wizards art or logos.
- **AI:**
  - Wizards' Generative AI FAQ forbids people who contribute to official D&D products from using generative AI in the final product. It covers Wizards' own products only. https://dnd-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/26243094975252-Generative-AI-art-FAQ
  - Hasbro CEO Chris Cocks told Semafor (2025-03-06) that AI is "supercharging fandom… net good for the brand." He also floated an AI-enriched campaign subscription service. https://www.semafor.com/article/03/06/2025/we-had-to-go-back-to-play-how-hasbros-dungeon-master-ceo-got-serious-about-games
  - He later said AI isn't used to make D&D books because the audience doesn't want it. https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/hasbro-ceo-chris-cocks-dnd-wont-use-ai
  - In October 2026, D&D and D&D Beyond staff joined the UWOTC-CWA union, demanding an enforceable AI policy. https://godisageek.com/2026/10/wizards-of-the-coast-dnd-studio-x-union-drive/
  - **Not found:** any Wizards statement about third-party LLM tools built on the SRD, or about users feeding purchased books to AI. I couldn't verify what D&D Beyond's terms of service say about AI or scraping.

- **What this means for our data model:**
  - Classes must be data, not code, since new classes keep arriving (Artificer, Psion).
  - Species need lineages.
  - Feats need types: Origin, General, Fighting Style, Epic Boon, Dragonmark, Dark Gift.
  - We also need to model evolving magic items, Bastions, Circle Magic, group patrons, professions and curses.

## 4. Ownership formats
- **How people own the books:**
  - Print hardcovers.
  - D&D Beyond digital: browser plus a mobile app that can download books for offline use but re-checks the license periodically.
  - Digital-plus-print bundles (the 2024 core three cost $179.97). https://marketplace.dndbeyond.com/beyond-digital/core-rulebook-bundle
- **No official PDFs of the 2024 books.** D&D Beyond moderators said there are no plans for them. https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/general-discussion/88605-download-pdf
- **No book export in any format (text, JSON or PDF) and no public API.** The 2026 D&D Beyond roadmap is a platform rebuild; an API is only a user request. https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2132-d-d-beyonds-2026-development-roadmap
- **Copy-paste from the D&D Beyond reader is unverified.** Some users say it's disabled on book pages; a staff member said copying for personal use is fine. https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/d-d-beyond-general/d-d-beyond-feedback/41812-content-from-books
- **Character sheet "Export to PDF" exists,** but users say it still produces the old 2014-style sheet for 2024 characters. https://dndbeyond-support.wizards.com/hc/en-us/articles/7747238449556-Export-Sheet
- **Virtual tabletop editions are separate purchases:** Foundry's official 2024 Player's Handbook module, Fantasy Grounds, Roll20. https://foundryvtt.com/packages/dnd-players-handbook
- **What the import feature must accept:**
  1. Pasted plain text or Markdown. This is the main path, covering D&D Beyond copy-paste and typing up print books.
  2. PDFs with a text layer: third-party and homebrew PDFs, and D&D Beyond character sheet PDFs.
  3. Photos or scans of print pages, read with OCR or Claude's vision.
  4. JSON in our own schema, for backups and sharing.
  - Foundry dnd5e JSON could be an optional extra, but its license terms are unverified.
  - The flow: Claude parses the input into structured records, the user reviews them, and they are stored only in the user's own browser.
